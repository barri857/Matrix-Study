Your learning. Your data.

Matrix privacy information · Updated November 19, 2026

Accounts and saved work

Guest work stays in your browser. With a Matrix account, your name, email, study kits, notes, plans, resources, and grades are stored in Matrix’s Cloudflare database so you can use them across devices. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Session records include an IP address and browser information to support secure sign-in.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google, Matrix requests your basic profile and email to create or access your account. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or your other Google content. Google credentials and sessions are handled by Google and Matrix’s account system.

Your AI provider

AI features use the provider and API key you select. Keys stay in this tab’s browser session unless you choose to remember them on the device. When you make an AI request, your key and the relevant study text, chat, or document page images are sent through Matrix to that provider. Keys are not stored in Matrix’s database or included in study exports. Your selected provider processes the request under its own privacy and usage policies.

Documents and public links

Files are prepared in your browser. Visual reading sends rendered PDF pages, PowerPoint slides, or uploaded images, together with extracted text, to your chosen AI provider. Extracted text and AI interpretations are saved when you save a study kit. Original files and rendered page images are not stored in Matrix’s database or workspace exports. Kits are private unless you explicitly create a public link. Anyone with that link can read the kit’s materials and study content; personal review history is excluded. You can stop sharing a kit.

Control and deletion

You can export your workspace and delete kits, notes, plans, or resources inside Matrix. Clearing browser storage removes guest data and remembered keys from that browser. For account deletion or privacy requests, contact barri857@gmail.com. Deleting browser data does not delete signed-in cloud data.

Services and retention

Matrix is independently hosted on Cloudflare. It uses essential sign-in cookies, browser storage, and operational service logs. Stored account work remains until you delete it or request account deletion. Service logs and backups may persist according to the hosting provider’s retention settings. Matrix does not add advertising trackers or sell your study content.
